# Shattered Ring — Environmental Reaction Pack

*Offline authored design reference — Phase 2 (M5) presentation pass. Not shipped game code.*

## Purpose

This pack drafts visible reaction language for the Shattered Ring's surfaces during major combat impacts — dust, flicker, structural creak, scuffing. It is presentation flavor only. No hazard, damage volume, destructible object, or physics object is described or implied anywhere below; none exist in Phase 1, and this pack does not add any.

## Reaction language, by surface

**Central combat floor**
On a major impact, the plating underfoot takes a visible surface scuff — a scrape of light across the panel seams — without altering the floor's function as an open, readable space for spacing, lock-on, dodges, counters, and Final Clash staging. The floor remains flat and clear; nothing rises from it, cracks open, or persists as an obstacle.

**Far doorway (Crimson Vanguard's entrance axis)**
A major impact can register as a faint structural creak along the doorway's frame — a settling groan in the metal, there and gone. The doorway itself does not move, shift, or change function; it remains the dedicated entrance axis and nothing else.

**Walls / overhead lighting**
Impacts near a wall can prompt a brief light flicker along the nearest fixture, and a light drift of dust shaken loose from an overhead beam or wall seam. Both are cosmetic — visible, then settled. Neither affects sightlines, silhouette readability, or the side-on framing the duel depends on.

## Framing notes (build-side status, not fiction)
