# Echo/Nova Impact Window Cinematic Beat Pack

## First Impact Window (Onboarding) — 0.75 s response time

The first Impact Window is deliberately generous: after a first successful perfect dodge or counter, one contextual timing prompt opens and holds for **0.75 s**. The burst never plays on its own — it is earned only when the player succeeds at that input. A miss does not get corrected for the player; combat simply resumes.

**Echo.** On success, Echo's exchange sharpens into a brief, controlled beat — a precise strike line, a measured guard angle, the kind of clean, deliberate timing that reads as mastery rather than haste. The burst plays out and combat resumes.

**Nova.** On success, Nova's exchange erupts into fast, aggressive momentum — quick footwork, an urgent close of distance, a strike thrown with speed rather than patience. The burst plays out and combat resumes.

## Standard Impact Window — 0.35–0.50 s response time

Once cooldown clears, later Impact Windows tighten to a **0.35–0.50 s** response time for any approved skill event. The prompt is briefer and less forgiving, but the rule doesn't change: success earns the burst, failure returns play to normal combat immediately, with no auto-correction and no extra punishment.

**Echo.** The tighter window suits Echo's identity — a narrow, exacting strike line delivered with the same controlled timing, the burst compressed but no less precise.

**Nova.** The tighter window suits Nova's identity too — momentum snapping forward in a sudden, aggressive burst, speed compensating for the shorter margin.

## Duration and framing (both fighters, both windows)

Every earned burst runs somewhere in the governed **1–3 second** range. Success at the timing input is what earns the burst — it does not set, extend, or otherwise determine how long that burst runs; duration is decided elsewhere and is not something the player's input controls. Whether the trigger was a perfect dodge, a counter, or an approved combo milestone, and regardless of which fighter is on screen, the beat plays out as a punishable opening against Crimson Vanguard's recovery, then the exchange returns to real-time combat.

## What this beat pack does not claim

These descriptions stop at "the burst ends and combat resumes." They do not assert how the rival's Behavior Tree, camera ownership, hitboxes, or animation state behave underneath that return — those restoration specifics are flagged **open** pending M3 sign-off and are out of scope for this content pack.

## Ascension Meter tie-in (reference only, values unchanged)

An Impact Window success also contributes to the Ascension Meter at the governed rate of **+20**, alongside perfect dodge (**+12**), successful counter (**+15**), and light-combo finisher (**+5**). Taking damage or waiting contributes **+0**. These values are quoted verbatim from the approved knowledge base and are not altered here.
