## The costume, as shown

**Silhouette.** Shorter and slighter than Echo, but **visually busier** — the layered
jacket, the cargo-cut trousers, and the thigh pouches break the outline where Echo's
is smooth. This matches GDD §07's "compact, agile layered profile".

**Head.** A full-face helmet with a **pale light-grey shell cap over the crown and
back**, and a **dark visor** across the face. A **small yellow-green indicator light**
sits at the lower side of the helmet. A **tall orange collar** stands up around the
neck.

**Torso — the layered system.** A **cropped jacket worn over a vest**, ending above
the waist and leaving a charcoal technical shirt visible beneath. Broad **orange
panels** run over the shoulders and down the chest either side of a centre zip. Small
**yellow-green indicator strips** sit on the chest and left ribs. The back view shows
a large orange shoulder yoke and a charcoal spine panel.

**Insignia.** A **circular badge on the upper arm**, orange-outlined, carrying a
stylised angular monogram. The sheet names it **"Unique 'SFN' Unit Insignia"** —
the only readable lettering identified on either fighter's sheet.

**Waist and legs.** A wide belt. **Cargo-cut trousers, loose to just below the knee**,
with **orange piping down the outer leg** and **large flapped thigh pouches on both
sides**. Below the knee the leg becomes a **close-fitting black lower legging**, so
the leg silhouette narrows sharply — quite unlike Echo's continuous line. **Segmented
knee armor** with a small yellow-green light. Grey chevron marks point down the outer
thigh.

**Hands.** Fingerless or partial gloves, charcoal with **orange fingers**.

**Feet.** **High-top sneakers** — black and charcoal upper, **orange accents, pale
grey/white midsole**, visible laces, and a swoosh-style side mark. A dedicated inset
shows the shoe in profile and its **sole tread from underneath** (orange lug pattern
with a circular pivot disc).
