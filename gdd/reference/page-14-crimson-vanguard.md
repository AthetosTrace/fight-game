## The design, as shown

**Silhouette.** Enormous and top-heavy. Rounded pauldrons sit above and outboard of
the shoulders, the chest is a broad forward-projecting plate, and the arms hang wide
and clear of the body. Thighs and boots are heavily plated. The proportion reads
**mech/powered-armor rather than human** — the head is small relative to the torso.

**Colour and materials.** **Glossy red armor plating over a matte dark-grey/black
substructure**, exactly the "red armor over black structure" of GDD §07. The red is
saturated and reflective with hard specular edges; the black underlayer is visible at
every joint — neck, armpit, elbow, waist, knee, ankle. Small **white/light rectangular
markings** appear on the right pauldron and on both knee caps.

**Head / optics.** A tapered helmet with a **raised central crest** and **two swept
horn-like blades** rising from the temples. **Two angular eye slots glow amber-orange**
with visible bloom. A small dark triangular vent sits at the brow. The face is fully
enclosed — no mouth, no visor slit, no human features.

**Chest / power core.** A layered chevron plate. At its centre sits a **bright
amber-white emissive core**, the brightest point on the figure. **Thin amber slits**
run along the ribs and abdomen either side of it.

**Left arm / gauntlet.** A **fully enclosed armored fist** — red segmented finger
plates over a heavy dark-grey wrist cuff carrying **two small amber indicator lights**.
There is no visible weapon barrel, blade, or muzzle: the hand *is* the weapon.

**Vents / thrusters.** The two untitled close-ups show a **louvred intake or exhaust
with amber light glowing between the slats**. Position on the body is not stated in
these insets — **AMBIGUOUS** — but the rear view shows matching units.

**Back / thrusters.** The rear view shows a **heavily plated back with large vertical
red vanes rising above the shoulders** and a segmented spine column. These are the
thruster forms the GDD's Attack D and Phase 2 text refer to.

**Action vignettes.** Two loose grey sketches, unrendered and uncoloured: one figure
**crouched low and driving forward** as if launching or landing; one **wide-stanced
and braced**, arms out. **AMBIGUOUS** — neither is labelled, and neither is tied to a
named attack.
