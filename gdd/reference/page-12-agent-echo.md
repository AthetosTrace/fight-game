## The costume, as shown

**Silhouette.** Lean and upright. Athletic human proportion with no bulk added
anywhere — the outline stays close to the body from shoulder to ankle. This is the
"lean, upright technical striker" silhouette of GDD §07.

**Head.** A **smooth, entirely black full-face helmet**, rounded at the crown and
tapering to a narrow chin. Glossy finish with a specular highlight. The face area is
one continuous dark surface — the sheet's own label, **"Visor or Light"**, leaves its
function undecided. A **standing orange collar** rises behind the jaw.

**Torso.** A one-piece **"Segmented Body Suit"** in matte black with charcoal panel
breaks over the chest, ribs, and abdomen. **Diagonal harness straps** cross the chest
and shoulders. A **shoulder badge** — an orange-outlined shield-like mark with a
white diagonal slash — sits on the left deltoid. The rear view shows **thin orange
energy lines** tracing up the spine and across the shoulder blades, converging on a
**central back unit**, the "Backpack Power Unit Core". The unit is low-profile and
integrated, not a worn pack.

**Waist and legs.** A **belt with small orange indicator lights** at the buckle line.
Black legs with a **long orange blade-shaped accent down the outer thigh** and a
matching stripe at the rear. **Hexagonal knee guards**, segmented at the side.

**Hands.** Charcoal gloves with **orange fingers** and small orange knuckle dots.

**Feet.** Low-profile black shoes with an **orange sole and toe wrap**. A dedicated
inset shows the **sole tread from underneath** — a heavy orange lug pattern with a
circular pivot disc at the ball of the foot and small printed text on the tread that
is too small to read (**AMBIGUOUS**).
