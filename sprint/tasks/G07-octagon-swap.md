## Goal

The fight happens in `Lvl_ArenaOctagon`, not the flat graybox.

## Why it matters

**Worth 3 rubric points, not just looks.** A10's Pipeline-to-Game Connection requires
shipped content traceably produced by the pipeline. The octagon was generated by
`game/Tools/ArenaPipeline/`, with run evidence already committed under
`game/reports/arena/`. Ship on the flat graybox and that evidence points at nothing in the
build. Ship in the octagon and the connection is demonstrable on video.

## Decided: one level, no sublevels — and the merge goes octagon → duel

**Revised 2026-08-27. The direction is the opposite of what this task first said.**

Copy the **octagon geometry into `Lvl_DuelGraybox`**, delete its flat plane, and rename the
level afterwards if wanted. Do **not** move the fighters into `Lvl_ArenaOctagon`.

Why this way round — three things stay untouched that would otherwise all need redoing:

1. **The cook set.** `GameDefaultMap` and `EditorStartupMap` already point at
   `Lvl_DuelGraybox`, and only maps reachable from `GameDefaultMap` get cooked. Merging into
   the duel level means the arena is in the build automatically, with no packaging config
   change at all.
2. **The Vanguard's per-instance overrides.** The placed instance carries a mesh
   `relativeScale3D` of (1.1, 1.1, 1.1) and the `ABP_VanguardLocomotion` anim class as
   *instance* values, not just class defaults. Re-placing the actor means re-applying both —
   and the blackboard records that exact stale-instance trap being hit repeatedly across
   §20, §21 and §22.
3. **PlayerStart and GameMode wiring.** Already correct in the duel level.

Moving geometry is safe; moving configured gameplay actors is where the bugs are.

Do not attach the duel as a streamed sublevel and do not keep two levels in play.

Unreal does support sublevels and level streaming, and that is roughly what "attaching a
level" describes — but for a single-arena, single-match game it buys nothing and costs
cross-level actor references, streaming states to debug, and extra packaging edge cases.
One level, one default map, one thing to open.

**The build scripts are generators, not a runtime dependency.** The octagon geometry is
already saved as 43 external actor packages committed in git. Opening the level shows the
arena immediately. `build_octagon_*.py` is re-run only to *change* the geometry, never to
see it.

## Preconditions

- `G05` complete — moving the fight is easier once the match loop is proven somewhere.

## Steps

1. Checkpoint first. `Lvl_ArenaOctagon` already has `CP01_ShellGood` and
   `CP02_TrussAndParapets`; add one more before placing gameplay actors.
2. **Close the arena-size question before placing anything.** The octagon is 1590 cm
   apothem (`build_octagon_tiers.py`); the duel clamps the player at ±650. So the fighters
   would use a 1300 cm strip inside a 3180 cm space. This is the unresolved U1 question
   from `game/docs/arena/ARENA_PIPELINE_FINDINGS.md` §4.2.
   **Cheapest resolution: keep ±650 and centre it in the octagon** — the surrounding space
   reads as arena depth, which is what a gallery is for. Widening the clamps means
   re-tuning spacing, camera framing and the Vanguard's approach, and re-validating all of
   it. Do not widen unless the fight visibly feels cramped. **Record the decision either
   way.**
3. Read spawn points from the level. Do not build on world origin.
4. Place the fighters, the camera rig, the knockout coordinator and the match director.
   **Re-apply the per-instance overrides** — Vanguard mesh `relativeScale3D` (1.1, 1.1,
   1.1) and the `ABP_VanguardLocomotion` anim class. The blackboard flags stale-instance
   bugs repeatedly; this is where they bite.
5. Lighting pass. The graybox is flat-lit; the octagon has a gallery, truss walls and ramp
   slabs that need light to read as geometry.
6. Verify collision: the ramps and gallery must not let the player leave the combat strip
   or get stuck on a slab edge.
7. Re-run the `G05` and `G06` acceptance checks in the new level. Neither is proven here
   until re-run.
